# Evidence and claims

Current state: repository setup only; research not started.

Authorship order: Grant Boudreaux first, Daniel Fredriksen second. Planned verification is LLM review and Lean certification under the Quantyra process. LLM review is not human peer review. Certification requires preserved evidence for exact theorem statements, hypotheses, source/dependency identities, kernel acceptance and axiom profiles; it does not establish novelty or broader claims on its own.

Source proposals, verified results, and open questions must be identified separately. Angle definitions, exceptional cases, domain restrictions, inverse conventions, and branches require validation before mathematical use.

Expressing Cartesian components as real and imaginary parts does not establish a holomorphic change of variable or a result about zeta zeros. Numerical checks are not proofs. A DOI archives an artifact and does not certify correctness, originality, peer review, or publication rights.

No formal or informal mathematical verification, literature review, experiment, or peer review has occurred in this repository. Any later result must include its exact statement, assumptions, evidence, and limitations.
